The short version We collect only what Kyro Chat needs to work. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. Text messages are end-to-end encrypted, but photos, voice notes, videos and files are not yet. You can delete your account at any time.

This policy explains what Vael Tech ("we", "us") collects when you use Kyro Chat, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, write to us at our contact email.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, date of birth (we also keep your age), and a password. We store your password only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Profile details you choose to add: username, bio and profile photo.
  • Your messages and media: text, photos, videos, voice notes, files, GIFs and stickers you send, plus polls, reactions, and the groups and communities you join.
  • Safety information: people you block, and reports you send us about other users.

Information created when you use the app

  • Whether you are online, your last-seen time, and typing or recording indicators. You can turn these off in Privacy settings.
  • One-time codes (OTP) sent to your email to verify your account or log you in, and a record of failed attempts so we can lock out guessing.
  • Basic technical data our servers and hosting providers see when you connect (such as IP address and request logs).

Information that stays on your device

  • Your encryption private key, app settings, drafts, saved messages, reminders, scheduled messages, starred messages, chat folders, and the search index that powers "Search messages". These are stored in your browser or app storage, not on our servers. Clearing your app data removes them.

2. How your messages are protected

  • Text messages in one-to-one chats are end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted on your device and can only be read on the recipient's device. We store and relay the encrypted version and cannot read it.
  • Photos, videos, voice notes and files are not end-to-end encrypted yet. They are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored on our servers, so our systems can access them.
  • Your encryption key lives on your device. If you clear your browser or app data, or sign in on a new device, you may not be able to read older encrypted messages.
  • Features that need to read a message's text, such as translation, only run on a message you choose (or a chat where you turn them on). See "Services we use".

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, verify it, and keep it secure.
  • To deliver your messages and calls, and show the right profile and status to the right people.
  • To keep Kyro Chat safe: handle reports, block abuse and spam, and enforce our Terms.
  • To fix bugs, keep the service running, and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your agreement first where the law requires it.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other users: your name, username, profile photo, bio, and (depending on your privacy settings) your online status and last seen. Messages go to the people you send them to.
  • Our team: only as needed to run, secure and support the service, and to review reports.
  • Authorities: if the law requires it, we may have to share information we hold. Because text messages are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot hand over their contents.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details are kept until you delete your account.
  • Messages are kept until they are deleted, expire (disappearing messages), or your account is deleted.
  • One-time codes expire within minutes and are removed after use.
  • We may keep limited records for a time where we need them to prevent abuse or comply with the law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Control who sees your last seen, online status, typing and read receipts in Privacy settings.
  • Block and report users from their profile.
  • Use disappearing messages, view-once media and app lock.
  • Edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete your account and data, or ask for a copy of your data, by emailing our contact email from the address on your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to correct, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

Kyro Chat is for people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it. In some places, people under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to use online services; you are responsible for meeting that requirement.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, hash passwords, protect logins with one-time codes and lock-outs after repeated wrong attempts, and encrypt text messages end to end. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your device locked.

10. Where your data is processed

Our servers and service providers may be in countries other than yours. By using Kyro Chat you understand that your information may be transferred and processed there.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make an important change we will update the date above and ask you to review and accept the new version when you next open the app.
